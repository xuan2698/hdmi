The ports are 8-bit `d`, 1-bit `reset`, and 8-bit `q`, all sampled on the positive edge of `clk`. Boundary values are `0x00` and `0xFF` for `d`/`q`, and `0`/`1` for `reset`. The checker should treat `q` as a single 8-bit word: at each sampled rising edge, `q_next = 0x00` if `reset` is high, else `q_next = d`. No per-bit mixing is allowed. Mid-row input changes occur after sampling and must not alter the expectation for the edge just sampled; they carry into the next row. Power-up is unspecified, so the checker should not assume an initial `q` unless the testbench states one. Worked example: `d=0xA5, reset=0` gives `q=0xA5`; next edge `d=0x3C, reset=1` gives `q=0x00`, not a mixed value.
