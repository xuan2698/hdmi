The most useful distinguishing example for the checker is the transition into `0x0099` followed by a deferred reset, because it simultaneously tests enable computation, partial carry, and row-boundary discipline.

Consider a prior sampled state `q = 0x0098` with `reset` low at the sampled rising edge. The new settled state is `q = 0x0099`. In this row the enables must be computed from the new `q`, not the prior one: `ena[0]` is high because the ones digit is 9; `ena[1]` is high because the tens digit is 9 and the ones digit is 9 (so the tens digit will increment on the next count); `ena[2]` is low because the hundreds digit is 0. The checker should verify these three bits against `q = 0x0099` alone, independent of how the state was reached.

Now suppose `reset` changes from low to high after this sampled edge, during the mid-cycle window. That change must not alter the `0x0099` row’s `q` or `ena`. It is carried to the next rising edge, where high reset forces `q = 0x0000`; the enables in that reset row are computed from the resulting `0x0000`, so all three are low. A checker that samples `reset` too late, or that recomputes the previous row’s enables after seeing the mid-cycle reset, will incorrectly flag this sequence. The correct pairing is: (prior sampled state `0x0098`, sampled reset low) → settled `q = 0x0099` with enables `{1,1,0}`; then (prior sampled state `0x0099`, sampled reset high) → settled `q = 0x0000` with enables `{0,0,0}`.

This example also pins down the rollover boundary separately: at `q = 0x9999` all three enables are high, and the next non-reset edge yields `0x0000`. The `0x0099` case is the sharper test because only two enables are high, so a checker that derives enables from a simple “any lower digit is 9” rule, or that uses the prior state’s digits, will fail here while still passing the all-9s case. The final checker should therefore capture `reset` at the sampled edge, capture settled `q` and `ena` after that edge, verify reset precedence, verify the BCD increment from the prior sampled state when reset is low, and compute `ena` solely from the current sampled `q`, deferring all mid-cycle input changes to the next row.
