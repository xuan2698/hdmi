The public interface is five 1-bit inputs (`areset`, `bump_left`, `bump_right`, `ground`, `dig`) and four 1-bit outputs (`walk_left`, `walk_right`, `aaah`, `digging`). All are scalar; there are no vectors, no multi-bit counters exposed at the ports, and no output enables. The internal fall counter is therefore invisible to the checker except through its behavioral consequences at the landing edge. `areset` is positive-edge-triggered asynchronous and forces the machine to the walk-left state; the checker should treat reset as immediately effective, not synchronized to `clk`, and should confirm that the first sampled edge after reset shows `walk_left=1` with the other three outputs low.

Boundary values matter in three places. First, the fall counter boundary is exactly 20 versus 21: a landing after 20 falling cycles is safe, a landing after 21 splatters. The checker must count falling sampled edges carefully, excluding the entry edge (ground disappears) and excluding the landing edge itself. Second, the direction-memory boundary is the three edge types where bumps must be ignored: the disappearance edge, any mid-fall edge, and the reappearance edge. Asserting both bump inputs on those edges and confirming the resumed direction equals the pre-fall direction is the cleanest way to catch a machine that accidentally lets a bump leak into direction memory. Third, the output invariant boundary is the non-splattered state space: exactly one of the four outputs is high in every non-splattered state, and all four are zero only after splatter. The checker should test this on the first post-reset edge and the first post-power-up edge, since a spurious splatter there would violate the invariant immediately.

Precedence is the other boundary the checker should probe. On a walking edge with ground present, `dig` outranks bump, and bump outranks nothing else; with `ground=0`, falling outranks both. A useful single-edge probe is walking right with `ground=1`, `dig=1`, `bump_left=1`: the machine should enter digging, retain right-direction memory, and discard the bump. The next edge with `ground=0` should end digging and enter falling with `aaah=1`, direction memory still right; bump assertions during the fall should change nothing; and a landing with fall count ≤20 should resume `walk_right=1`. The checker should confirm the direction never flipped and exactly one output was asserted throughout. After splatter, all four outputs must remain zero across subsequent edges until `areset`, and the checker should verify that no mid-air cycle splatters regardless of count.
