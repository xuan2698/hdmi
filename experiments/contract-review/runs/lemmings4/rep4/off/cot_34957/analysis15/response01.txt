The key checker-relevant structure is that the machine has two independent pieces of memory: a walking-direction latch and a fall-duration counter. Both are cleared by `areset`, but only the counter is cleared on a safe landing; the direction latch survives the entire fall and is consumed on landing. This separation is what makes the "resume same direction" rule testable.

The direction latch is written at the moment ground disappears while walking (or while digging, which also carries a direction). It is not written on the disappearance edge from a non-walking state, and it is not written during falling. Bumps on the disappearance edge, during the fall, and on the reappearance edge are ignored for direction purposes. On landing, the stored direction is restored and the counter is zeroed. The checker should assert both bump inputs on those three edge types and confirm the resumed direction equals the pre-fall direction.

The fall counter starts at zero on reset and on every safe landing. It increments once per sampled edge while in the falling state, but not on the edge that enters falling (ground disappears) and not on the landing edge itself. Splatter is evaluated only on the landing transition: a count of exactly 20 is safe, 21 or more splatters. Mid-air cycles never splatter regardless of count. After splatter, all four outputs are zero forever until `areset`. The checker should drive exactly 20 fall cycles then land and confirm `walk_*` resumes; then drive 21 fall cycles then land and confirm all outputs stay zero across subsequent edges.

Precedence on a walking edge with ground present: dig outranks bump, and bump outranks nothing else. If `ground=0`, falling outranks both dig and bump. If digging and `ground=0`, the machine transitions to falling with direction memory intact. Digging while falling or with no ground is ignored.

Outputs are Moore-style: exactly one of `walk_left`, `walk_right`, `aaah`, `digging` is asserted in every non-splattered state, and all four are zero only after splatter. The checker should verify this invariant on every sampled edge, including the first post-reset edge and the first post-power-up edge, confirming no spurious splatter occurs there.

Worked example: walking right with `ground=1`, `dig=1`, `bump_left=1` on one edge. Dig outranks bump, so the machine enters digging, retains right-direction memory, and discards the bump. Next edge `ground=0`: digging ends, machine falls (`aaah=1`), direction memory still right. Falling edges with `bump_right=1` change nothing. Then `ground=1` with fall count ≤20: machine resumes `walk_right=1`. The checker confirms the direction never flipped and exactly one output was asserted throughout.
