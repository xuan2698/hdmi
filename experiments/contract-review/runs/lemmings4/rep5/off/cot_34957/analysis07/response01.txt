The key timing distinction for the final checker is between the sampled edge and the optional mid-cycle input changes. Each row's inputs are driven before the sampled posedge; the machine's outputs are read after that edge has settled. Any mid-cycle changes occur after sampling and before the opposite edge, and they carry into the start of the next row. So the checker should treat each row as one Moore evaluation: compute next state from the current state plus the row's inputs, then emit outputs for that next state. Mid-cycle changes must not be folded into the same row's output; they only affect the following row's inputs.

For the fall counter, the important edge is the transition into falling, not the row where `ground` first reads 0. On the disappearance row the Lemming is still walking (fall has lower precedence than walking only when ground is present; once ground=0, fall takes over), but the counter should begin counting on the subsequent falling cycles. The landing row is where `ground=1` and the previous state was falling; that is the only place splatter can be decided. A 20-cycle fall must survive; a 21-cycle fall must splatter. Off-by-one errors here are the most likely failure mode, so the checker should verify the counter is incremented once per full falling cycle and tested exactly at the falling-to-grounded transition.

Bumps during falling or digging are ignored for direction. Bumps on the disappearance cycle and on the reappearance cycle also do not change direction. Dig has lower precedence than fall but higher than direction switching: if `ground=1`, not falling, and `dig=1`, enter digging; digging continues until `ground=0`, then falls, then resumes the original walking direction on landing. Splatter overrides everything and is permanent until `areset`.

Reset is asynchronous and positive-edge triggered: it forces walk-left, clears the fall counter, and clears any splatter-pending latch. A stale doomed flag surviving reset is a classic bug.

Worked example: walking right, `ground=0` for 21 sampled cycles, then `ground=1`. The disappearance row still shows walking right; the next 21 rows are falling with `aaah=1`; the landing row must output all zeros (splatter). If the counter were incremented on the disappearance row, a 20-cycle fall would wrongly splatter.
