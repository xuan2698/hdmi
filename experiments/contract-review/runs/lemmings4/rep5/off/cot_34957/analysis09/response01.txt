The machine's externally visible behavior is a Moore output function of an internal state that must encode at least: walking direction, falling status, digging status, splattered status, and a fall-duration counter. Reset forces the state to walk-left, not falling, not digging, not splattered, with the fall counter cleared. Because reset is asynchronous and positive-edge triggered, the checker must apply it at the event time specified by the protocol, not defer it to the next clock edge; the next sampled row is then evaluated from the reset state. No test should assume an unknown or don't-care power-up state: absent an explicit reset, the expected outputs are walk-left with all other outputs low.

Walking behavior: while walking on ground and not digging, exactly one of walk_left/walk_right is high. A bump on the left forces walk-right; a bump on the right forces walk-left; simultaneous bumps still cause a direction switch. Bumps have no effect while falling or digging. Digging begins only when dig=1, ground=1, and the Lemming is walking (not falling); it continues until ground=0, at which point digging ends and falling begins. Precedence among simultaneous conditions is fall > dig > direction switch: if ground=0, the Lemming falls regardless of dig or bumps; if ground=1 and dig=1 while walking, digging starts rather than a bump-induced turn.

Falling behavior: when ground=0 the Lemming enters falling, asserts aaah, and increments an internal fall counter each cycle. While falling, bumps and dig are ignored. When ground returns, the Lemming resumes walking in the direction it had before the fall, and the fall counter is cleared. If the fall duration exceeded 20 cycles at the moment of landing, the Lemming splatters: all four outputs become 0 permanently until reset. Splattering occurs only on landing, never mid-air. A bump in the same cycle that ground disappears, or in the same cycle ground reappears while still falling, does not alter direction.

Digging behavior: digging asserts digging=1 and suppresses walking outputs. Bumps are ignored. When ground becomes 0, digging stops and the Lemming falls; after landing it resumes walking in its original direction. Dig commands while falling or with ground=0 are ignored.

The critical checker point is reset completeness. Reset must clear the walking direction to left, clear falling/digging/splattered flags, and zero the fall counter. A stale counter or splatter latch surviving reset is a bug: a Lemming reset mid-fall must not later splatter from pre-reset fall history. Tests should include reset during a long fall and reset after a near-splatter fall, then confirm walk-left and that a subsequent short fall lands safely. Also test that a mid-cycle asynchronous reset changes the next sampled row immediately, without waiting for a clock edge.
