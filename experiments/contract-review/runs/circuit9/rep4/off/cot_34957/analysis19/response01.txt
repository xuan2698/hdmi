The critical timing detail is that the first rising edge is not a reset or load event. The circuit powers up in state 4, and the first edge simply applies the ordinary rule: since `a = 1`, it holds 4. A checker that treats the first edge specially—for example, forcing `q = 4` on the first rising edge regardless of `a`—would match this trace but would be wrong for any test where the first sampled `a` is 0. The wrap from 6 to 0 at 75 ns confirms the modulus is 7, not 8. A plain 3-bit incrementer would produce 7 at that edge and diverge afterward. The update must be `(q + 1) % 7`.
